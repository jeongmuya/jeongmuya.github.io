Climbing Finger Training

Tendon10

Tendon10 is a minimal finger training timer for climbers. It helps you follow a simple 10-minute routine for hangboard or no-hang training.

Training Routine

  • 10 seconds of work
  • 50 seconds of rest
  • 10 total sets
  • Local training history tracking

Privacy

Training history is stored locally on the device. Tendon10 does not require sign-in or server synchronization.
